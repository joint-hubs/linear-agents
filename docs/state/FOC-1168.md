## 2026-11-03 06:58 — ## The fix — tick-lineage remembered map (over the Job Object alternative)

`scripts/supervisor-lib.mjs`:
- `rememberChildLineage(entries, rootPid, remembered, {minCreationMs, maxCreationMs, childCreation})` — one tick's merge into the remembered map (`pid → creation`). The snapshot must still contain the child's own row (a snapshot without it was taken after the child died — a reuse of the child's pid could sit there vouching for strangers); once the child's creation stamp is known, later ticks verify it, so a reuse between ticks stops the walk instead of poisoning the map. Returns the child's creation stamp for the caller to carry into the next tick.
- `descendantsInProcessTable(entries, rootPid, {minCreationMs, maxCreationMs, remembered})` — two new acceptance rules behind the existing fail-safe window: (1) IDENTITY — a row whose `pid + creation` a tick verified while the child was alive joins even though its parent chain died since; a reused pid fails the identity exactly; (2) the DEAD-INTERMEDIATE BRIDGE — a row whose parent is remembered but no longer in the snapshot joins when born after that parent and inside the child's spawn window; a live verified parent is left to the unchanged chain rule; when the parent's pid is now held by a stranger, a row born after the stranger's own creation was spawned by the stranger and is refused. Identity is `pid + creation` everywhere — a reused pid is still refused, and every refusal is a NOT-killed.
- `reapChildDescendants` gains a `remembered` pass-through.

`scripts/supervisor-watch.mjs`: a lineage scanner off the event path (timer, never the stdout handlers; sync execFileSync with windowsHide + timeout inside `enumerateWin32Processes`): one tick 1.2 s after the spawn, then every 60 s, win32 only, skipped silently once the child exited or the turn finalized; a skipped or failed enumeration is a note (`lineage_scan_skipped` / `lineage_scan_failed`), never a crash. finalizeTurn passes the map to the reap.

AC-2 (the ticket's real rows): the `bash (dead) → env.exe → node --test` chains are covered because bash waited on env and env waited on the 4-hour suite — every tick saw all three alive together, so all three are remembered and the suite joins by identity (unit test pins this exact shape). A short-lived intermediate that no tick saw alive (and whose children no tick saw either) stays a documented safe miss — stated in the `descendantsInProcessTable` docblock, whose FOC-1162 "died AND reused" wording is corrected here.

Approach decision: tick-lineage, NOT the Job Object. The Job Object (P/Invoke holder, no native module) kills unconditionally-reachable trees but needs one holder process per child, a new Win32 FFI surface in the watcher, and it changes the kill semantics from verified-refuse to assigned-ownership: a child that exits its job (or a job assignment race at spawn) is exactly the fail-open direction the FOC-1162 review refused. Tick-lineage keeps the existing verify-then-kill shape, reuses the FOC-1162 machinery, and its residual (an intermediate no tick saw) is a miss, never a wrong kill.

## 2026-11-03 07:33 — ## REVIEW r1 fix — last-seen-alive bound on the dead-intermediate bridge

Shape chosen: the reviewer's FIRST direction — a per-pid last-seen-alive bound, bridge only rows born at or before it. Why this one and not refusing-the-band-plus-words: the bound makes every accepted row provable instead of documenting an unprovable one. The proof chain: a tick snapshot that carries the parent's row (pid + creation identity verified) proves the parent alive at that snapshot moment; seenMs is captured BEFORE the enumeration, so it is never later than the snapshot moment; a row born at or before seenMs was necessarily born by whoever held the pid then, and the identity stamp pins that holder to our process. A row born after seenMs has unprovable parentage — the parent may have been dead for ticks, its pid transiently held by a stranger that spawned the row and exited (the reviewer's free-pid sub-case) — and is refused regardless of whether the reuse is still visible at finalize (the visible-reuse sub-case was additionally covered by the round-1 holder rule).

The stamp does NOT advance on a tick that could not re-verify the row (pinned by unit test) — so a reuse visible to a later tick cannot extend the bridge band. A sampling-gap stranger (born, spawned a child, and died entirely between two adjacent ticks) stays unprovable-by-sampling and is now named as the residual in the docblock, next to the existing never-seen-intermediate miss; both remain safe misses, never a wrong kill.
